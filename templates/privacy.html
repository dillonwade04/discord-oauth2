Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how S.U.N.D.A.Y. (“we,” “us,” “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Discord bot (“Service”).

2. Information We Collect

  • Server Data: server IDs, names, and roles.
  • User Data: Discord user IDs, usernames, and badge/status data.
  • Command Inputs: free-text or option data submitted to bot workflows.
  • OAuth Data: Discord access and refresh tokens needed to provide authorized features.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To enable core moderation, logging, background-check, leave, badge, dual-clan, and OAuth features.
  • To troubleshoot issues and improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Storage Locations: PebbleHost, Render, and the Bot Operator’s personal PC.
Retention Period: Data is kept while you remain on the CSSO server or until you request deletion.

5. Third-Party Access

PebbleHost and Render have access solely to host and run the bot. We do not sell or rent your data.

6. Consent & Deletion

You may request that we remove your data from active systems. Withdrawing consent also removes access to features that require that data.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If discovered, that data will be deleted.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date will change when material updates are made.

10. Contact Us

For questions or deletion requests, send a direct message to S.U.N.D.A.Y. on Discord.
